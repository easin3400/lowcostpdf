How to Reduce PDF Printing Cost in Bangladesh

Printing costs are high for students and offices in Bangladesh. One simple solution is printing multiple PDF pages on one sheet. This can reduce your paper usage by up to 75%.

Key saving: A 100-page PDF printed 4-up uses only 25 sheets — saving 75% paper and money.

Why Printing Is Expensive in Bangladesh

  • Paper price has increased significantly in recent years
  • Ink and toner costs add up quickly for large documents
  • Students frequently reprint notes and assignments

Best Page-Per-Sheet Settings

  • 4 pages per sheet → best for students (saves 75% paper)
  • 2 pages per sheet → best for small text or diagrams
  • 6 pages per sheet → best for presentation slides

Cost Example

A 100-page PDF: normal print → 100 sheets. 4-up print → 25 sheets. That is 75% paper saving, which translates directly to lower printing bills at any copy shop in Bangladesh.
