Best PDF Print Layout for University Notes

University students print long PDFs every semester — lecture slides, past papers, reading lists and assignments. Using the correct layout makes revision easier and significantly cheaper.

Recommended: 4 pages per sheet for most university notes. Saves 75% paper while keeping text readable.

Why 4-Up Is the Best Layout for University Notes

  • Readable — 11–12pt text stays clear at 4-up
  • Easy to carry — a semester's notes fit in one folder
  • Low printing cost — 75% less paper than 1-up
  • Revision-friendly — see more content per page

When to Use 2-Up Instead

Use 2 pages per sheet for: math or engineering diagrams, scanned handwritten notes, PDFs with small font, or documents you need to annotate.
