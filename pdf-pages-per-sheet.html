Which Layout Should You Choose?

For Students (School, College, University)

The 4 pages per sheet layout is the most popular choice for students in Bangladesh. It cuts your printing cost by 75% while keeping the text readable for revision. A 100-page PDF becomes just 25 A4 sheets.

If your PDF has very small text, use 2 pages per sheet instead — you'll still save 50% and the text will be comfortable to read.

💡 Student Tip: For university lecture slides where each slide has large headings and a few bullet points, 4-up printing is perfect. For engineering or medical textbooks with dense text and diagrams, 2-up is safer.

For Teachers and Coaching Centers

When printing class handouts, 4-up or 6-up layouts dramatically reduce your printing budget. For a batch of 50 students and a 40-page handout, you go from 2,000 sheets down to just 500 (4-up) or 334 (6-up).

For Offices and Businesses

For reference materials, meeting agendas, and internal documents, 2-up or 4-up printing reduces costs without sacrificing readability. Use 2-up for documents people need to write annotations on.

How N-Up Printing Works

N-up printing (also called "multiple pages per sheet") rearranges the pages of your PDF onto fewer sheets by shrinking each page and placing multiple of them on a single A4 or letter-sized page. The original content is unchanged — only the size is reduced.

LowCostPDF.xyz does this entirely inside your browser. No internet upload is needed — your PDF is processed locally using PDF-lib, a JavaScript library that runs on your own device.

Is 4-Up Printing Always Readable?

It depends on your original PDF's font size and layout. As a general rule:

  • PDFs with 12pt+ body text → 4-up is comfortable to read
  • PDFs with 10–11pt text → 4-up is tight; consider 2-up
  • Slide-deck PDFs (large fonts, few words) → 6-up or 9-up is fine
  • Scanned PDFs with handwriting → use 2-up to keep it legible

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about N-up PDF printing

What is N-up printing?

N-up printing means placing N pages of a document onto a single printed sheet. For example, 4-up means 4 pages on one sheet (saving 75% paper). The term "N-up" comes from the printing industry.

How do I print multiple pages per sheet on any printer?

Use LowCostPDF.xyz to convert your PDF to an N-up layout, then open the converted PDF and print normally with your printer set to A4. No special printer settings are needed — the layout is already built into the output PDF.

Which layout saves the most paper?

The 16-up layout saves ~94% of paper but text becomes very small. For practical use, 4-up (75% savings) offers the best balance of paper savings and readability.

Does this work with scanned PDFs?

Yes. LowCostPDF.xyz works with any PDF — scanned, image-based, or text-based. For scanned PDFs with handwriting, we recommend 2-up to keep the writing legible.

Can I print different page counts per sheet in a single document?

Currently, LowCostPDF.xyz applies a single layout to the entire document. If you need different layouts for different sections, split your PDF first, convert each part, then combine.
