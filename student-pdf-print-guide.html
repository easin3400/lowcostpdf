Student Guide: How to Print PDF Notes Efficiently

Students print large amounts of PDFs for exams and revision every semester. Using the right print layout can save significant money and paper throughout the academic year.

Quick tip: Switching from 1-up to 4-up printing saves 75% of your paper — and money — on every print job.

Best Layout for Students

4 pages per sheet is ideal for most lecture notes, past papers and handouts. It keeps text readable while cutting paper usage by 75%.

When to Use 2 Pages Per Sheet

  • Small font size in the original PDF
  • Math or engineering diagrams that need detail
  • Scanned handwritten notes
  • Documents you will annotate or write on

Common Student Mistake

Printing one page per sheet wastes paper. Always check the pages-per-sheet setting before sending to print. A 200-page PDF printed 1-up costs 4× more than the same file printed 4-up.
