A4 PDF Printing Guide – Pages Per Sheet Explained

A4 is the standard paper size in Bangladesh, India, the UK and most of the world. Choosing the right pages-per-sheet layout for A4 printing makes a big difference in cost and readability.

A4 Recommended Layouts

  • 2-up (2 pages per sheet): Best readability — ideal for detailed documents
  • 4-up (4 pages per sheet): Best balance — most popular for student notes
  • 6-up (6 pages per sheet): Good for slides and visual presentations
  • 9-up / 16-up: Maximum density — for reference sheets only

Which Layout Should You Choose?

For most A4 PDF documents with standard (11–12pt) font size, 4 pages per sheet is the best choice. It keeps text readable while reducing paper use by 75%.
