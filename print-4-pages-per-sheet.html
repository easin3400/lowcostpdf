Print 4 Pages Per Sheet (2×2 PDF Layout)

Printing 4 pages per sheet is the most popular way to reduce paper usage while keeping text readable. This method places four PDF pages in a 2×2 grid on one A4 sheet.

75% paper saved: A 100-page PDF becomes just 25 printed sheets.

Why 4 Pages Per Sheet Is the Best Choice

  • Saves 75% paper compared to normal printing
  • Perfect for school, college and university notes
  • Readable for most PDFs with standard font sizes
  • Lower cost at print shops — fewer sheets means lower bill

Who Should Use 4-Up Printing?

Students use 4-up printing for exam preparation and lecture notes. Teachers use it for handouts. Office workers use it for internal documents and reference material.

Is 4-Up Printing Always Readable?

If your PDF uses 11–12pt text, 4-up is readable for most people. For very small text, scanned PDFs, or math diagrams, use 2-up instead.
