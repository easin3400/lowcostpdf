Increase Pages Per Print: Print More PDF Pages on One Sheet

To increase pages per print, use a pages-per-sheet layout. This lets you fit 2, 4, 6, 9 or 16 PDF pages on one A4 sheet.

Direct answer: The best way to increase pages per print is to use 4 pages per sheet. It gives a strong balance between readability and paper saving.

What Does “Increase Pages Per Print” Mean?

It means printing more than one PDF page on a single physical sheet of paper. For example, instead of printing 1 page per sheet, you can print 4 pages per sheet.

Pages Per Print Options

  • 2 pages per print: Better readability, moderate saving.
  • 4 pages per print: Best balance for study notes.
  • 6 pages per print: Good for presentation slides.
  • 9 pages per print: Compact preview layout.
  • 16 pages per print: Maximum density, smallest text.

Best Setting for Most Users

For most students, 4 pages per print is the best setting. It turns a 100-page PDF into only 25 printed sheets.

When Should You Use 2 Pages Per Print?

Use 2 pages per print when your PDF has:

  • Small text
  • Scanned handwriting
  • Math formulas
  • Engineering or medical diagrams
  • Documents that need annotation

When Should You Use 6, 9 or 16 Pages Per Print?

Use higher page counts for visual previews, presentation slides, reference sheets and rough paper copies. Do not use 16 pages per print for serious reading unless the original PDF has very large text.
