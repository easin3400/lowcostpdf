Low Print Cost Hack: Save Money When Printing PDFs

The simplest low print cost hack is to print multiple PDF pages on one A4 sheet. Instead of printing one PDF page per paper sheet, you can print 2, 4, 6, 9 or 16 pages per sheet.

Quick answer: Print 4 PDF pages per sheet to reduce paper use by 75%. A 100-page PDF becomes only 25 printed A4 sheets.

How This Printing Hack Works

Multiple-pages-per-sheet printing shrinks each PDF page and places several pages on one printable sheet. This is also called N-up printing.

Example:

  • Normal print: 100 PDF pages = 100 paper sheets
  • 2-up print: 100 PDF pages = 50 paper sheets
  • 4-up print: 100 PDF pages = 25 paper sheets
  • 6-up print: 100 PDF pages = about 17 paper sheets

Best Low Cost Setting: 4 Pages Per Sheet

For most people, 4 pages per sheet is the best low-cost setting. It saves a lot of paper but still keeps text readable.

Who Can Benefit From This?

  • Students: lecture notes, exam PDFs, university handouts
  • Teachers: class materials and question sheets
  • Coaching centers: bulk handout printing
  • Offices: internal reports, meeting documents and drafts

Low Print Cost Strategy for Students

Students often print long PDFs before exams. Instead of printing everything normally, first prepare the PDF with a 4-up layout. This makes the notes smaller, cheaper and easier to carry.

Important Readability Rule

Do not always choose the highest number of pages per sheet. 16 pages per sheet saves the most paper, but it may be too small to read. For real study use, 4-up is usually better than 9-up or 16-up.
